Tyre change without the queue

We take your car to its tyre change, tyre hotel or wheel-alignment visit and return it ready for the season.

Request a price Call +358 50 357 2836
Price: from 149 € Passengers: None, you do not travel with it Appointment: Recommended Service area: Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Kauniainen

You do not need to travel with the car. The driver takes it to the destination and carries no passengers.

How it runs

  1. You confirm the slot, or that the provider accepts the car without an appointment.

  2. You tell us where the tyres are: at the provider, in the car, or at another address.

  3. We collect the car, document its condition and deliver it to the tyre provider.

  4. We return the car to the agreed address once the provider releases it.

Included in the DriveMe service

  • Pickup and return
  • Delivery to the named tyre provider
  • Handover status and completion record
  • Reasonable waiting only when included in the selected product

Customer responsibilities

  • Confirm that the provider accepts the vehicle at the requested time
  • State whether tyres are at the provider, in the vehicle or at another collection address
  • Pay the tyre provider directly
  • Disclose wheel-lock key location

Not included

  • Tyres, fitting, storage or alignment charges
  • Transporting loose tyres unless explicitly accepted
  • Assessing tyre safety
  • Queue time beyond the included allowance

Responsibility boundary

The customer chooses the tyre provider and pays it directly. DriveMe is responsible for collection, driving, handover and return.

Vehicle eligibility and refusal rules

  • Valid registration, insurance and legally required inspection.
  • Correct seasonal tyres and safe tyre condition.
  • Sufficient fuel or battery to complete the planned route plus reasonable reserve.
  • No active driving ban, unsafe damage, serious fluid leak or critical warning.
  • Normal controls compatible with the assigned driver’s licence and disclosed competence.
  • Keys and any wheel-lock key provided as needed.

A driver will not drive a vehicle that appears unsafe, illegal or materially different from the booking information. We document the situation, pause the job, and the customer arranges towing or another legal transport method. Only the base fee and incurred time stated in the terms are charged.

Price

Tyre service run: from 149 €, VAT included. A typical job in the capital region is 149–199 €, depending on route, timing and waiting. You see an indicative price on the request form immediately, and we confirm a fixed DriveMe fee before the driver is sent. The provider’s own charge, such as the inspection or the service, is paid directly to the provider.

Frequently asked

Can you take the car without a booked slot?

Only if your chosen provider accepts walk-ins and you accept a possible waiting charge. In peak season we require a booked slot, because queues make a return time impossible to promise.

Do you carry loose tyres?

Only when separately agreed. Tell us in the booking whether the tyres are in the car, at the provider or at another address.

Do you assess my tyres?

No. We are not a tyre shop and do not judge tyre safety - that is for the provider you choose.

Who pays for the tyre work?

You pay the provider directly. The DriveMe fee covers the vehicle movements only.

Service area

We currently serve Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Kauniainen. Longer moves are priced individually.

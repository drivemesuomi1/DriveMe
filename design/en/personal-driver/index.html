A personal driver for your own car

Your vehicle, your plans, a professional driver - for an evening, an event or a multi-stop day.

Register interest Call +358 50 357 2836
Price: 39 €/h Appointment: Not needed Service area: Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Kauniainen

Not yet bookable

This service is awaiting regulatory and insurance confirmation. We take expressions of interest, but we do not confirm jobs before the licensing and insurance position is confirmed in writing.

How it runs

  1. You tell us the schedule, pickup, stops and destination.

  2. We confirm the driver and the price before the drive.

  3. The driver arrives at the agreed time and drives your own car.

  4. The car and keys are handed back to you at the agreed place.

Included in the DriveMe service

  • Driver time under the confirmed schedule
  • Agreed pickup, stops and destination
  • Documented driver identity and support contact
  • Safe handover of vehicle and keys

Customer responsibilities

  • Be fit to enter and use the vehicle safely as a passenger
  • Provide a legal, insured and roadworthy vehicle
  • Disclose special controls
  • Pay parking, tolls and vehicle energy unless the quote says otherwise

Not included

  • Transporting an unaccompanied child
  • Medical supervision or assisted-care obligations
  • Driving an unsafe vehicle
  • Unlimited waiting or unconfirmed extensions

Responsibility boundary

Driver service in the customer’s own car requires regulatory and insurance clearance. We do not market it as available until those confirmations are in writing.

Vehicle eligibility and refusal rules

  • Valid registration, insurance and legally required inspection.
  • Correct seasonal tyres and safe tyre condition.
  • Sufficient fuel or battery to complete the planned route plus reasonable reserve.
  • No active driving ban, unsafe damage, serious fluid leak or critical warning.
  • Normal controls compatible with the assigned driver’s licence and disclosed competence.
  • Keys and any wheel-lock key provided as needed.

A driver will not drive a vehicle that appears unsafe, illegal or materially different from the booking information. We document the situation, pause the job, and the customer arranges towing or another legal transport method. Only the base fee and incurred time stated in the terms are charged.

Price

Personal driver: 39 €/h, VAT included. You see an indicative price on the request form and we confirm a fixed DriveMe fee before the driver is sent. Third-party charges are not included - you pay them directly to the provider you choose.

Frequently asked

Does the driver use my car or yours?

Yours. That is the point of the service: a familiar car, your own child seats and belongings, no transfer into a strange vehicle.

Is the service available now?

Passenger transport in the customer’s own car is awaiting regulatory and insurance confirmation. We take expressions of interest but do not confirm jobs until that is confirmed in writing.

Is there an hourly minimum?

The recommended minimum is two hours. We state the price and minimum with your booking.

Do you transport a child alone?

No. Unaccompanied child transport is excluded from the service.

Service area

We currently serve Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Kauniainen. Longer moves are priced individually.

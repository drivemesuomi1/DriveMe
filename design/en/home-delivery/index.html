Delivering a sold car to your customer

We deliver a sold car from your showroom to the customer’s agreed address. Collection, delivery time and handover practice are agreed in advance. It also suits customers buying from another town.

Request a price Call +358 50 357 2836
Price: Fixed quote Passengers: None, you do not travel with it Appointment: Not needed Service area: Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Kauniainen

You do not need to travel with the car. The driver takes it to the destination and carries no passengers.

How it runs

  1. Tell us the collection point, the buyer’s contact details and a realistic delivery window.

  2. We confirm the price, the driver and the delivery time before the drive.

  3. The driver checks the visible condition at collection and records the odometer.

  4. The car and keys are handed to the named receiver, and you get a confirmation of the handover.

Included in the DriveMe service

  • An agreed delivery window and contact with the buyer before arrival
  • Visible condition and odometer recorded at collection
  • Handover of the car and the keys to the named receiver
  • Confirmation of the handover sent to you afterwards

Customer responsibilities

  • Remain responsible for the sale documents, registration and what was said about the car
  • Provide the buyer’s contact details and agree a realistic delivery window
  • Tell us in advance if the handover includes a demonstration or a trade-in collection

Not included

  • Demonstrating the car or explaining its technical condition to the buyer
  • Collecting the purchase price or any other payment
  • Trade-in collection without a separately agreed second transfer

Responsibility boundary

DriveMe is responsible for the transfer and the documented handover. The selling dealer remains responsible for the terms of sale, the documents and any representations about the car. The receipt acknowledges delivery; it does not waive consumer rights.

Vehicle eligibility and refusal rules

  • Valid registration, insurance and legally required inspection.
  • Correct seasonal tyres and safe tyre condition.
  • Sufficient fuel or battery to complete the planned route plus reasonable reserve.
  • No active driving ban, unsafe damage, serious fluid leak or critical warning.
  • Normal controls compatible with the assigned driver’s licence and disclosed competence.
  • Keys and any wheel-lock key provided as needed.

A driver will not drive a vehicle that appears unsafe, illegal or materially different from the booking information. We document the situation, pause the job, and the customer arranges towing or another legal transport method. Only the base fee and incurred time stated in the terms are charged.

Price

Delivery to the customer: Fixed quote, VAT included. You see an indicative price on the request form immediately, and we confirm a fixed DriveMe fee before the driver is sent. Any fuel, parking or toll costs are stated separately in the quote.

Frequently asked

Which areas do you deliver to?

The capital region and Uusimaa are the standard area. Longer deliveries in Finland are agreed per route, once the driver’s return leg is settled.

Does the buyer need to be present?

Yes, or a receiver they have named. The handover is signed off by the person you told us about.

What about documents and keys?

The driver delivers the documents and keys given to them, as they are. We do not complete or sign sale documents on the dealer’s behalf.

Can you collect a trade-in at the same time?

We can, but it is its own transfer: a separate vehicle, its own condition check and its own line in the quote.

Service area

We currently serve Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Kauniainen. Longer moves are priced individually.

How DriveMe works

Every booking is confirmed by a person. That is slower than an automatic receipt, but it means every confirmed job has a real driver, a real time and a real price.

The customer journey

  1. Choose a path

    I need a driver, or take care of my car.

  2. Choose the service and shape

    One-way, pickup and return, or wait and return.

  3. Give addresses and timing

    Collection, provider or destination, return address and appointment details.

  4. Give vehicle details and accept the statements

    Registration, transmission, fuel type, plus eligibility and authorisation.

  5. See the indicative price and send the request

    A request does not yet bind either side.

  6. DriveMe confirms driver, time and fee

    Only the confirmation makes the booking binding.

  7. Collection is documented

    Keys, condition, mileage and fuel or charge level in timestamped photos.

  8. You receive status updates

    Every handover is notified.

  9. Return or delivery is signed off

    You or your authorised recipient confirms receipt.

  10. You receive a receipt or invoice

    Plus a short rating request.

What we need from you

  • Contact: name, phone, email, plus company details and Business ID for invoiced accounts.
  • Service: type, one-way or with return, date, collection window and required delivery time.
  • Locations: collection, provider or destination, return address, access and parking instructions.
  • Appointment: provider, confirmed time, booking reference, contact and key-drop method.
  • Vehicle: registration, make, model, year, transmission, fuel type, mileage and special controls.
  • Eligibility: roadworthy, insured, registered, inspected, no driving ban, known faults.
  • Handover: owner or keeper authority, pickup and delivery contacts, key method, documents.
  • Payment: DriveMe payment method, third-party paid directly, parking and fuel treatment, invoice details.

A request is not a confirmation

Submitting the form creates a request. The job is confirmed only when you receive our confirmation of the driver, time and fixed fee.

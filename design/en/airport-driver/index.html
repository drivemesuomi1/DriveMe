Own-car airport driver

Travel to or from the airport in your familiar vehicle, with luggage and child-seat setup already yours.

Register interest Call +358 50 357 2836
Price: from 129 € Appointment: Flight details Service area: Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Kauniainen

Not yet bookable

This service is awaiting regulatory and insurance confirmation. We take expressions of interest, but we do not confirm jobs before the licensing and insurance position is confirmed in writing.

How it runs

  1. You give the flight details, passenger count and luggage.

  2. We agree where the car and keys end after departure or begin before arrival.

  3. We confirm the driver, the schedule and the price.

  4. The driver takes you to the airport and handles the car as agreed.

Included in the DriveMe service

  • Pre-booked driver and agreed support logistics
  • Flight/time details
  • Customer and vehicle handover according to the selected option
  • Airport drop-off fees stated in the quote

Customer responsibilities

  • Provide accurate flight and luggage information
  • Ensure the vehicle is suitable and legal
  • Agree where the car and keys end after departure or begin before arrival
  • Provide child seats and install them personally

Not included

  • Airport parking unless quoted
  • Flight concierge or baggage handling
  • Vehicle storage
  • Traditional airport transfer in a DriveMe-owned car until that product is licensed and active

Responsibility boundary

This is a launch gate, not an automatic launch item. We confirm no jobs before written regulatory and insurance clearance.

Vehicle eligibility and refusal rules

  • Valid registration, insurance and legally required inspection.
  • Correct seasonal tyres and safe tyre condition.
  • Sufficient fuel or battery to complete the planned route plus reasonable reserve.
  • No active driving ban, unsafe damage, serious fluid leak or critical warning.
  • Normal controls compatible with the assigned driver’s licence and disclosed competence.
  • Keys and any wheel-lock key provided as needed.

A driver will not drive a vehicle that appears unsafe, illegal or materially different from the booking information. We document the situation, pause the job, and the customer arranges towing or another legal transport method. Only the base fee and incurred time stated in the terms are charged.

Price

Airport driver: from 129 €, VAT included. You see an indicative price on the request form and we confirm a fixed DriveMe fee before the driver is sent. Third-party charges are not included - you pay them directly to the provider you choose.

Frequently asked

Where does my car go while I fly?

It is agreed in the booking. The usual options are returning it to your home or an agreed address, or collecting it on your return day. Storage is not part of the service.

Is the service available now?

No. It awaits written Traficom and insurer confirmation on passenger transport in the customer’s own car.

Is the airport drop-off fee included?

It is stated separately in the quote when it applies to your journey.

Service area

We currently serve Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Kauniainen. Longer moves are priced individually.

Glass, body shop, recall and specialist runs

From a windscreen appointment to a manufacturer recall, DriveMe handles the roadworthy vehicle movement and handover.

Request a price Call +358 50 357 2836
Price: from 99 € Appointment: Required Service area: Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Kauniainen

How it runs

  1. You book the glass, body shop or dealer appointment and deal with your insurer yourself.

  2. You give us the booking or claim reference and the destination contact.

  3. We collect the car, document its condition and hand it over at the destination.

  4. We return the car once the provider releases it.

Included in the DriveMe service

  • Delivery and/or collection
  • Booking-reference handover
  • Condition documentation
  • Return when released by the provider

Customer responsibilities

  • Have a confirmed appointment
  • Deal directly with insurer and repairer
  • Provide claim or booking reference
  • Approve all costs and work

Not included

  • Insurance claim administration
  • Damage assessment
  • Towing accident-damaged or unsafe vehicles
  • Repair advice or warranty decisions

Responsibility boundary

The customer deals with the insurer and repairer and approves the work. DriveMe moves and hands over a roadworthy vehicle.

Vehicle eligibility and refusal rules

  • Valid registration, insurance and legally required inspection.
  • Correct seasonal tyres and safe tyre condition.
  • Sufficient fuel or battery to complete the planned route plus reasonable reserve.
  • No active driving ban, unsafe damage, serious fluid leak or critical warning.
  • Normal controls compatible with the assigned driver’s licence and disclosed competence.
  • Keys and any wheel-lock key provided as needed.

A driver will not drive a vehicle that appears unsafe, illegal or materially different from the booking information. We document the situation, pause the job, and the customer arranges towing or another legal transport method. Only the base fee and incurred time stated in the terms are charged.

Price

Glass, body shop and recall: from 99 €, VAT included. You see an indicative price on the request form and we confirm a fixed DriveMe fee before the driver is sent. Third-party charges are not included - you pay them directly to the provider you choose.

Frequently asked

Can you move a collision-damaged car?

Not if it cannot be driven safely. We do not accept a visibly unsafe, leaking, collision-damaged or warning-critical vehicle for a driven transfer - we route it to a towing partner.

Do you handle the insurance claim?

No. The claim, damage assessment and repair decisions are between you and your insurer. We handle the movement and handover.

Is a confirmed appointment required?

Yes, for glass, body shop and recall work.

Service area

We currently serve Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Kauniainen. Longer moves are priced individually.

Vehicle relocation: a driver takes your car to another address

We collect your roadworthy car and drive it to the agreed address, and back again if needed. You do not need to travel with it, and the handover is documented at both ends.

Request a price Call +358 50 357 2836
Price: from 89 € Passengers: None, you do not travel with it Appointment: Not needed Service area: Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Kauniainen

You do not need to travel with the car. The driver takes it to the destination and carries no passengers.

How it runs

  1. You give the collection and delivery addresses and the contacts at both ends.

  2. We confirm a fixed price from the route, the timing and the driver’s return logistics.

  3. We collect the car and document condition, mileage and fuel or charge level.

  4. We deliver the car and record the handover to the authorised recipient.

Included in the DriveMe service

  • One-way driven relocation
  • Pickup and delivery photos
  • Mileage, fuel/charge and key handover records
  • Status notifications

Customer responsibilities

  • Provide authorised contacts at both ends
  • Ensure valid registration, insurance, tyres and roadworthiness
  • Declare faults, modifications and special controls
  • Pay fuel, charging, tolls and parking as stated in the quote

Not included

  • Transport by truck or trailer
  • Moving deregistered or driving-banned vehicles
  • International movement unless expressly quoted
  • Storage

Responsibility boundary

DriveMe is responsible for the driven move and the documented handover. Vehicle eligibility and disclosure are the customer’s responsibility.

Vehicle eligibility and refusal rules

  • Valid registration, insurance and legally required inspection.
  • Correct seasonal tyres and safe tyre condition.
  • Sufficient fuel or battery to complete the planned route plus reasonable reserve.
  • No active driving ban, unsafe damage, serious fluid leak or critical warning.
  • Normal controls compatible with the assigned driver’s licence and disclosed competence.
  • Keys and any wheel-lock key provided as needed.

A driver will not drive a vehicle that appears unsafe, illegal or materially different from the booking information. We document the situation, pause the job, and the customer arranges towing or another legal transport method. Only the base fee and incurred time stated in the terms are charged.

Price

Vehicle relocation: from 89 €, VAT included. A typical job in the capital region is 99–129 €, depending on route, timing and waiting. You see an indicative price on the request form immediately, and we confirm a fixed DriveMe fee before the driver is sent. Any fuel, parking or toll costs are stated separately in the quote.

Frequently asked

Do I need to travel in the car?

No. The driver drives the car alone and carries no passengers. The keys just need to be handed over as agreed at collection and someone receives the car at the destination, or the keys are left at an agreed place.

Can I also book the return?

Yes. Say in the price request that the car should come back later, and we price the collection and return as one job.

Is the car driven or trailered?

We drive it. That is faster and cheaper for a roadworthy car. If the car cannot legally or safely be driven, it needs a trailer and we point you to a transport partner.

Do you move cars outside the capital region?

Yes, but long-distance moves are priced individually because the driver’s return trip is part of the cost. Ask for a quote on the route.

Who pays fuel and tolls?

They are stated separately in the quote. We do not hide them in the price.

Can someone else receive the car?

Yes. Name an authorised recipient in the booking and we record the handover to them.

Service area

We currently serve Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Kauniainen. Longer moves are priced individually.

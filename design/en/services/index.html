Vehicle transfers and service runs

A driver takes the car where it needs to go. Company transfers are priced by route; single moves and service runs follow the published price list.

Transfers for the motor trade

Branch-to-branch moves, deliveries of sold cars and collections of purchased ones. Every transfer leaves a documented handover.

  • Branch-to-branch transfer

    We move roadworthy vehicles between your sites on an agreed schedule: stock moves, test-drive cars, rental and company vehicles. Your staff keep doing their own work while DriveMe does the driving.

    Fixed quote

  • Delivery to the customer

    We deliver a sold car from your showroom to the customer’s agreed address. Collection, delivery time and handover practice are agreed in advance. It also suits customers buying from another town.

    Fixed quote

  • Purchased vehicle collection

    When your business buys a car from a private seller, another dealer or an auction, DriveMe can handle the collection and the transfer. We agree the handover practice with the seller and deliver the roadworthy car to the address you give.

    Fixed quote

Service transfers and single moves

A car to a booked service, inspection, tyre change or wash and back - or a move from one address to another.

  • Service transfer

    DriveMe collects a roadworthy car from the agreed address, takes it to the service point you choose and returns it as agreed. It suits company fleets, workshop customers and private owners alike.

    from 149 €

  • Vehicle relocation

    We collect your roadworthy car and drive it to the agreed address, and back again if needed. You do not need to travel with it, and the handover is documented at both ends.

    from 89 €

Vehicle moves and service runs

A driver takes your car where it needs to go. You do not need to travel with it, and you always choose the provider.

Take my car to a service

Collection, delivery to the provider you choose, and return as agreed. You book and pay the provider yourself.

  • Vehicle inspection run

    Already booked the inspection? We collect your roadworthy car, take it to the station and return it with the inspection documents.

    from 169 €

  • Workshop run

    Keep your appointment and keep your day. DriveMe handles the vehicle handovers while you deal directly with your chosen workshop.

    from 149 €

  • Tyre service run

    We take your car to its tyre change, tyre hotel or wheel-alignment visit and return it ready for the season.

    from 149 €

  • Wash and detailing run

    Your car gets its appointment without taking over your calendar.

    from 149 €

  • Glass, body shop and recall

    From a windscreen appointment to a manufacturer recall, DriveMe handles the roadworthy vehicle movement and handover.

    from 149 €

  • Dealer and lease handover

    DriveMe manages the physical handover; the customer keeps the purchase, lease or rental agreement directly with the provider.

    from 99 €

Drive my car to another address

A roadworthy car from one agreed address to another, and back again if needed.

  • Vehicle relocation

    We collect your roadworthy car and drive it to the agreed address, and back again if needed. You do not need to travel with it, and the handover is documented at both ends.

    from 89 €

  • Pickup and return

    One trusted partner for vehicle movements: we collect your roadworthy car, take it to the provider or address you choose and return it as agreed.

    from 149 €

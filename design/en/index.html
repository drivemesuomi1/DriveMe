How a transfer runs

  1. Ask for a quote

    Tell us your name, your details and which service you need. The form takes under a minute.

  2. We contact you within 24 hours

    We go through the addresses, the vehicle details and the schedule, and confirm a fixed price. An enquiry does not confirm a booking yet.

  3. The driver collects your car

    We photograph condition, mileage and fuel or charge level. You do not need to travel with it.

  4. Delivered, or back home

    Every handover is recorded, and you hear when the car is delivered or returned.

Frequently asked

Do I need an appointment?

Yes for inspection, workshop, glass, body shop and recall work. For a wash or tyres, only if the provider does not accept walk-ins; queue time may be charged.

Who pays the provider?

The customer pays directly. The DriveMe fee covers only the confirmed DriveMe service.

Can the driver approve repairs?

No. The provider must contact the customer for all approvals.

What if the car cannot be driven?

DriveMe refuses or pauses the job, and the customer arranges towing or legal transport.

How is condition documented?

Timestamped pickup and delivery photos plus mileage and fuel or charge level.
